1. Who is responsible

DYFWAS: Destroy Your Friends With Awesome Stuff ("the game") is developed and published by Pierrick Mahy, an independent developer working under the name Pouglin, based in Belgium — "we" in this text. We are the controller for the processing described here.

Contact for any question or request about your data: pouglin@gmail.com.

Valve Corporation (Steam) is an independent controller for your Steam account, purchases, friends list, Steam networking and everything Steam shows or stores. The Steam Privacy Policy applies to that processing.

2. Data stored on your PC

Your save files — settings, progress, unlocks, outfits, painted skins, custom bonuses, custom levels and match rules — are stored on your PC, in %USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\Pouglin\DYFWAS - Destroy Your Friends With Awesome Stuff. We do not receive these files.

Some information about what you do in the game can still leave your PC, but only in the cases described below: what other players need to see during an online match (§ 3), the statistics if you accept them (§ 4, for example "item X was unlocked"), a bug report you choose to send (§ 5), and the creations you choose to share with other players (for example a custom bonus code). Other players can choose to hide custom art made by others in their own options.

3. Steam and multiplayer

The game uses Steam to identify you, to create and join lobbies, and to connect players. When you play online, the game uses your Steam ID, Steam name and avatar to show you in the lobby and in matches; other players in your lobby see your Steam name and avatar, and their copy of the game receives your Steam ID. Game data (movements, shots, choices, your outfit and creations used in the match) is exchanged between the players' PCs through Valve's relay network (Steam Datagram Relay), which the game uses for every online connection; with this relay, other players do not receive your IP address. We run no game server and do not record matches.

4. Pseudonymous gameplay statistics — optional

To balance the game and fix problems, the game can send pseudonymous statistics to a small service we run on Cloudflare. Nothing is sent until you agree: the game asks once, nothing is pre-selected, and declining takes nothing away from the game. You can change your mind anytime in Options → Gameplay → Privacy.

What the statistics contain, if you agree:

  • a random identifier created on your PC when you agree. It is not derived from your Steam account. It can however be linked to your Steam account if you also send a bug report, because bug reports include it (§ 5);
  • game events: matches played, modes and settings, bonuses offered and picked, weapons used, results, deaths described by slot, weapon and cause (other players are never identified), unlocks and items equipped, menu steps;
  • technical information: game version, operating system, processor, graphics card, memory, screen resolution, language, smoothness (frames per second), errors.

Not included in the statistics: your Steam ID, your Steam name, other players' names, chat, free text, your precise position on screen.

Your IP address: like any connection on the Internet, our service sees your IP address when your PC sends statistics. It is used only for a few moments, to limit the number of requests (protection against abuse), and is not recorded in our database; request logging is turned off for this service. Cloudflare, which runs the infrastructure, may process connection data for security under its own terms.

Why: to spot unbalanced bonuses or weapons, understand where players get stuck, fix crashes and slowdowns. We do not use it for advertising, do not sell it, and do not try to identify you.

Retention: 12 months at most, then deleted automatically.

Your control:

  • Export my data saves a copy of the statistics linked to your identifier as a file.
  • Turning sharing off (or Delete my data, which does the same) stops all sending immediately, empties the queue of events not yet sent, and deletes the statistics already received from our service. If your PC is offline at that moment, the deletion request is kept on your PC and sent automatically as soon as you are online again. If you accept again later, a new identifier is created.

5. Bug reports

The bug report window (F8 key) sends a report only when you click Send. A report contains:

  • your description and a screenshot (you can untick the screenshot);
  • the game's log file for the session. Before sending, the game replaces your Windows user name with <user> wherever it appears (for example in file paths);
  • technical information: game version, operating system, processor, graphics card, memory, display, language, time zone, input devices, performance;
  • the game state: scene, mode, online role;
  • your Steam ID and Steam name, so we can find related reports and, if needed, recognise you if you contact us;
  • your statistics identifier, if you accepted statistics, so we can look at the statistics around the problem.

Reports go through our Cloudflare relay to a private channel of our Discord server, which only we can access. We use them only to fix the game. We delete each report at the latest 12 months after we receive it, and earlier once the problem is fixed and the report is no longer needed.

6. Writing to us

If you write to us, we receive your e-mail address, your message and any attachments. We use them to answer you and, when your message reports a problem or a suggestion, to improve the game. They are kept in our e-mail account (Google Gmail) and deleted 2 years after our last exchange, or sooner on request.

7. This website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub records the IP addresses of visitors for security purposes, under its own terms (GitHub Pages data collection). This page uses no cookies, no analytics and no trackers of ours.

9. Recipients, international transfers, security

We do not sell or rent any data and show no advertising. Data is communicated only to the recipients described above, for the purposes described above:

  • Cloudflare runs the statistics service and the bug-report relay on our behalf, under its data processing terms;
  • Discord stores the bug reports in our private channel, and Google (Gmail) stores our e-mails; both provide these services under their own terms and privacy policies;
  • Valve (Steam) and GitHub (this website) act under their own policies.

These companies may process data outside the European Economic Area, notably in the United States. Such transfers rely on the safeguards each provider applies, such as the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework for companies certified under it, or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. You can write to us (§ 1) to know the safeguard that applies to a given provider.

All exchanges are encrypted in transit (HTTPS, or Steam's encrypted networking). Access to the statistics, the relay and the bug-report channel is limited to our accounts. If a data breach happens, we will notify the supervisory authority and the people concerned where the law requires it.

10. Deleting your data

  • Statistics: Options → Gameplay → Privacy → turn sharing off or Delete my data (see § 4).
  • Data on your PC: uninstall the game and delete the folder %USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\Pouglin\DYFWAS - Destroy Your Friends With Awesome Stuff. Do not delete the whole Pouglin folder if you play other games by Pouglin.
  • Bug reports and e-mails: write to us (§ 1), from the Steam account or e-mail address concerned if possible, so we can find them.
  • Steam data: managed by Valve, from your Steam account.

11. Your rights

Under the GDPR (European Union, EEA) you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection (in particular to processing based on legitimate interest), data portability, and the right to withdraw your consent at any time without affecting what was done before. These rights apply under the conditions set by the GDPR. Residents of other countries have the rights granted by their local law, which we handle the same way.

The Export and Delete my data buttons are a convenience; you can always write to us instead (§ 1). We answer within one month of receiving your request (this can be extended by two months for complex requests, in which case we tell you why), and we carry out the request we accept without undue delay. Because the statistics are pseudonymous, we may need information from you (for example the details of a bug report you sent) to find the data concerned.

You may lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority: in Belgium, the Data Protection Authority (APD/GBA); elsewhere in the EU/EEA, your country's authority; in the United Kingdom, the ICO; in Switzerland, the FDPIC.

United States residents: we do not sell or share personal information for targeted advertising, and the game shows no ads.

12. Age

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data about them. Gameplay statistics rely on consent: below the age at which a person can give that consent alone in their country (13 in Belgium; up to 16 in some EU countries), statistics may only be turned on with the agreement of a parent or legal guardian. Otherwise, leave them off; the game works the same. If you are a parent and believe your child has sent us data, contact us (§ 1) and we will delete it.

13. Changes

Any change will be published on this page with its date. Significant changes (for example a new kind of data sent) will be announced in the game before they take effect and, where required, your consent will be asked again.
