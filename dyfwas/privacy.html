1. Who is responsible

DYFWAS: Destroy Your Friends With Awesome Stuff ("the game") is developed and published by Pouglin, an independent developer (an individual) based in Belgium — "we" in this text. We are the controller for the processing described here.

Contact for any question or request about your data: pouglin@gmail.com. Given our size, we have not appointed a data protection officer: we handle requests ourselves.

Valve Corporation (Steam) is an independent controller for your Steam account, purchases, friends list, Steam networking and everything Steam shows or stores. The Steam Privacy Policy applies to that processing.

2. Data stored on your PC

Your settings, progress, unlocks, outfits, painted skins, custom bonuses, custom levels and match rules are stored only on your PC (in the game's folder under AppData\LocalLow\Pouglin). We receive none of it.

If you share a custom bonus code or a custom creation with other players, you choose what you share and with whom. Other players can hide custom art from others in their own options.

3. Steam and multiplayer

The game uses Steam to identify you, to create and join lobbies, and to connect players. When you play online, the game uses your Steam ID, Steam name and avatar to show you in the lobby and in matches; other players in your lobby see your Steam name and avatar. Game data (movements, shots, choices) is exchanged between the players' PCs through Steam's networking, which relays the connection. We run no game server and do not record matches.

4. Pseudonymous gameplay statistics — optional

To balance the game and fix problems, the game can send pseudonymous statistics to a small server we run on Cloudflare. Nothing is sent until you agree: the game asks once, nothing is pre-selected, and declining takes nothing away from the game. You can change your mind anytime in Options → Gameplay → Privacy.

What is sent, if you agree:

  • a random identifier created on your PC when you agree, unrelated to your Steam account, and reset if you withdraw your agreement;
  • game events: matches played, modes and settings, bonuses offered and picked, weapons used, results, deaths described by slot, weapon and cause (other players are never identified), unlocks and items equipped, menu steps;
  • technical information: game version, operating system, processor, graphics card, memory, screen resolution, language, smoothness (frames per second), errors.

What is never sent: your Steam ID, your Steam name, other players' names, your IP address (our server does not store it), chat, free text, your precise position on screen.

Why: to spot unbalanced bonuses or weapons, understand where players get stuck, fix crashes and slowdowns. We do not use it for advertising, do not sell it, and do not try to identify you.

Retention: 12 months at most, then deleted automatically.

Your control: in the same Privacy section, Export my data saves a copy of your statistics as a file, and Delete my data erases them from our server.

5. Bug reports

The bug report window (F8 key) sends a report only when you click Send. A report contains: your description, a screenshot (you can untick it), the game's log file for the session, technical information (game version, operating system, processor, graphics card, memory, display, language, time zone, input devices, performance), the game state (scene, mode, online role), your Steam ID and Steam name (so we can contact you or find related reports), and your statistics identifier if you accepted statistics.

Reports go through our Cloudflare relay to a private channel of our Discord server, readable only by us. We use them only to fix the game, and delete them at the latest 12 months after the problem is fixed.

6. Writing to us

If you write to us, we receive your e-mail address, your message and any attachments, only to answer you and improve the game. They are kept with our e-mail provider (currently Google Gmail) and deleted 2 years after our last exchange, or sooner on request.

8. Recipients, international transfers, security

We do not sell, rent or share any data. Our processors are Cloudflare (statistics server and bug-report relay), Discord (storage of bug reports) and Google (e-mail). Valve (Steam) acts as an independent controller. These companies may process data outside the European Economic Area, notably in the United States; such transfers rely on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and/or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

All exchanges are encrypted in transit (HTTPS or Steam's encrypted networking). Access to the statistics and reports is reserved to our accounts, protected by strong authentication. In the event of a data breach affecting you, we will notify the authority and, where required, the people concerned, within the legal deadlines.

9. Deleting your data

  • Statistics: Options → Gameplay → Privacy → Delete my data (and turn sharing off).
  • Data on your PC: uninstall the game and delete the AppData\LocalLow\Pouglin folder for the game.
  • Bug reports and e-mails: write to us (§ 1); we delete them within one month and confirm it to you.
  • Steam data: managed by Valve, from your Steam account.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR (European Union, EEA) and similar laws (United Kingdom, Switzerland, and other countries), you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, data portability, and the right to withdraw your consent at any time without affecting what was done before. Most can be exercised directly in the game (§ 4, § 9); for anything else, write to us (§ 1). We answer within one month. We will never treat you differently for exercising a right.

You may lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority: in Belgium, the Data Protection Authority (APD/GBA); elsewhere in the EU/EEA, your country's authority; in the United Kingdom, the ICO; in Switzerland, the FDPIC.

United States residents: we do not sell or share personal information for targeted advertising, and the game shows no ads.

11. Age

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data about them. Where the age of digital consent is higher (up to 16 in the EU), younger players should ask a parent before accepting statistics. If you believe a child has sent us data, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes

Any change will be published on this page with its date. Significant changes (for example a new kind of data sent) will be announced in the game before they take effect and, where required, your consent will be asked again.
